=============================

YENI DOSYA

DEGISEN

                  Eski hali: server_v80_backup.py

1) F12 AG KAYDI   [V81_NET]  : statik + Chrome(MITM) tum document/iframe/xhr/script/medya/altyazi
                                istekleri; method, status, ct, boyut, POST govdesi.
2) SABLON AJAX    [V81_TEMPLATE_FOUND/PARAM/CALL/RESULT]
                   fetch(`${ajaxurl}?action=..&post_id=${postId}`) gibi istekler:
                   degiskenler data-* / getAttribute / dataset / JS global / wp ajaxurl'den cozulur,
                   istek GERCEKTEN atilir, cevaptaki iframe/medya takip edilir.
3) EMBED TAKIBI   [V81_EMBED_FOLLOW] : .mp4/embed gibi gorunup HTML donen adres player sayfasi olarak acilir.
4) PACKER MEDYA   [V81_UNPACKED_MEDIA] : packer'dan cikan m3u8/mp4 statik dogrulanir
                   (V75+ tarayici runtime kapali oldugu icin bunlar bosa dusuyordu).
5) ALTYAZI        [V81_SUBTITLE_FOUND] / [V81_SUBTITLE] / [V81_SUBTITLE_SUMMARY]
                   <track>, tracks:[{file,label,kind}], Playerjs "[Turkce]url,[English]url",
                   subtitle/caption/altyazi alanlari, duz/goreli .vtt/.srt/.ass/.ttml,
                   packer ici, HLS #EXT-X-MEDIA TYPE=SUBTITLES, Chrome ag trafigi.
                   Thumbnail/sprite VTT'ler elenir. Her altyazi indirilip WEBVTT/SRT dogrulanir.
                   Bulunamazsa hangi kaynaklarin tarandigi yazilir (hardsub ihtimali).
6) REKLAM         [V81_HLS_DURATION] [V81_AD_REJECT] [V81_AD_MEDIA]
                   /ads/ /preroll/ /vast/ yolu ya da <60 sn ENDLIST'li HLS final SECILMEZ.
7) HLS DETAY      [V81_HLS_VARIANTS] [V81_HLS_AUDIO] [V81_HLS_KEY] : kaliteler, ses dilleri, AES anahtari.
8) HEADER TESTI   [V81_HEADER_TEST] [V81_PLAY_HEADERS]
                   Final manifest ve ilk segment, header'lar TEK TEK cikarilarak denenir:
                   REQUIRED / OPTIONAL. Tekrar acilamiyorsa "token/IP baglantili" uyarisi.
9) KAYNAK ZINCIRI [V81_STEP n] (provenance)
                   Final URL'den geriye: hangi cevabin icinde gectigi (PLAIN / JSON_ESCAPED /
                   PACKER / BASE64 / AJAX_TEMPLATE / HLS_MASTER_VARIANT / REFERER_ONLY),
                   istek header'lari, POST govdesi, set-cookie, snippet ve "extract" ipucu.
10) KARAR         [V81_VERDICT] READY | PARTIAL | FAILED
                  [V81_STOPPED_AT] basarisizsa nerede koptu:
                     TOP_PAGE_BLOCKED, REQUEST_BLOCKED, AJAX_TEMPLATE, ONLY_AD_MEDIA,
                     MEDIA_SEEN_NOT_PROVEN, PLAYER_FRAME_NO_MEDIA, IFRAME_FOUND_NOT_LOADED, NO_PLAYER_FOUND
                  [V81_MISSING] eksik parcalar (NONE ise eklenti icin her sey logda)
11) MAKINE OKUR   [V81_REPO_JSON] tek satir JSON (steps, params, final, headers, subtitles, ads, missing)
